# What Folders needs on the computer to do everything it offers; a line is a Debian package and what it is for.
bubblewrap          # the box strangers' archives, SVG drawings, PDFs and films are read in
ffmpeg              # thumbnails and details of films and sounds (ffmpeg and ffprobe)
poppler-utils       # thumbnails of PDF pages (pdftoppm)
udisks2             # drives: mounting and ejecting
cifs-utils          # mount.cifs: Windows and Samba shares open as ordinary folders
nfs-common          # mount.nfs: NFS exports opened by address (nfs://server/export) show as ordinary folders
smbclient           # lists the shared folders on a Windows or Samba computer
openssh-client      # ssh and sftp places
sshfs               # sftp places as ordinary folders
libarchive13t64     # reads RAR, disc images and the other archives Folders has no reader of its own for
xdg-utils           # xdg-open
trindle-toolkit-lang
